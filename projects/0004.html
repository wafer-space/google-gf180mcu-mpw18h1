Analog front-end for sensor readout (OpenFASoC)

0004 · Analog and mixed-signal · 1 × 1 slot

Rendered from the layout at 2.5 µm per pixel. Layers: NwellDiffusionPolysiliconMetal 1Metal 2Metal 3Metal 4Metal 5Top metalPad openings

248 logic cells: too few for density figures, which are given for designs of 1,000 or more.

Every figure here is measured from the public layout, the same way for every design on the run: see data and method, and how its designs used the process.

Analog front-end for reading out flexible laser-induced graphene sensors: a 14-bit SAR ADC, folded-cascode op-amps with programmable bias and bias generation, designed with open-source tools by Brown University. Published as A. Li et al., IEEE Solid-State Circuits Magazine 16(2), 2024 (doi:10.1109/MSSC.2024.3380586).
